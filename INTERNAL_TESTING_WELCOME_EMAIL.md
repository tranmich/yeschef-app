## 🎯 **What is YesChef?**

YesChef is a personal recipe manager and meal planning app designed to help families:
- 📖 Organize and preserve their favorite recipes
- 🔗 Import recipes from any website with one click
- 📅 Plan weekly meals effortlessly
- 🛒 Generate smart grocery lists automatically
- 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Share and collaborate with family members

## 📱 **How to Get Started**

### **Step 1: Download TestFlight**
1. Download **TestFlight** from the App Store (it's Apple's official beta testing app)
2. Open the app and accept the terms

### **Step 2: Install YesChef**
1. Check your email for the TestFlight invite
2. Click the invite link (or I'll share it shortly!)
3. Tap "Install" in TestFlight
4. Launch YesChef and sign up

### **Step 3: Start Testing!**
1. Create your account
2. Import your first recipe (try from your favorite food blog!)
3. Create a meal plan for the week
4. Generate a grocery list
5. Explore and have fun! 🎉

## 📋 **What We're Looking For**

### **High Priority Feedback:**
- 🐛 **Bugs:** App crashes, features not working, errors
- 🤷 **Confusing UX:** Anything that doesn't make sense
- 📱 **Performance:** Slow loading, laggy interactions
- 🔒 **Login Issues:** Problems signing in or staying logged in

### **Nice to Have:**
- 💡 **Feature Ideas:** What would make this better?
- 🎨 **Design Feedback:** Colors, layout, typography
- 📝 **Content Issues:** Typos, unclear labels
- 🌟 **Things You Love:** We want to know what's working!
