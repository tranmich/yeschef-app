1. Introduction

Welcome to YesChef. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and related services.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Email address, username, and password when you create an account
  • Profile Information: Custom avatar selections and user preferences
  • Recipe Data: Recipes you save, create, or share with the community
  • Meal Plans: Your meal planning preferences and schedules
  • Grocery Lists: Shopping lists you create and manage
  • Community Content: Posts, comments, and recipes you share

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Usage Data: How you interact with our application
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, application version
  • Performance Data: Crash reports and application performance metrics

2.3 Information from Third Parties

  • Authentication Services: Profile information when you sign in with Google or other third-party services
  • Payment Processing: Subscription status and payment information via RevenueCat when premium features are activated

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information for the following purposes:

  • Provide Services: Enable core application functionality including recipes, meal planning, and grocery lists
  • Personalization: Customize your experience with saved preferences and recommendations
  • Community Features: Enable recipe sharing and social interactions with other users
  • Account Management: Authenticate your account and provide customer support
  • Service Improvement: Analyze usage patterns to enhance application performance and features
  • Premium Features: Manage subscription status and premium feature access
  • Communication: Send important updates about your account or our services

4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4.1 When We May Share Information

  • With Your Consent: When you explicitly choose to share content publicly
  • Service Providers: With trusted service providers including Railway (cloud hosting), Expo (mobile platform), and RevenueCat (subscription management)
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect our rights and safety
  • Business Transfers: In connection with mergers, acquisitions, or asset sales

4.2 Community Features

  • Recipe Sharing: Content you choose to share publicly is visible to other users
  • Friend Connections: Your username and shared recipes are visible to accepted friends
  • Custom Avatars: Your selected avatar appears with your shared content

5. Data Storage and Security

5.1 Security Measures

  • Encryption: Data transmission is encrypted using industry-standard protocols
  • Authentication: Secure login with hashed passwords
  • Access Controls: Limited access to personal data by authorized personnel only
  • Regular Updates: Security patches and updates are applied promptly

5.2 Data Storage

  • Primary Storage: Your data is securely stored on Railway cloud infrastructure
  • Local Storage: Some preferences are cached locally on your device for offline access
  • Backup Systems: Regular backups ensure data recovery capabilities

6. Your Rights and Choices

6.1 Account Control

  • Access: View and update your profile information at any time
  • Data Portability: Request a copy of your data in a portable format
  • Deletion: Delete your account and associated data through the application settings

6.2 Privacy Settings

  • Profile Visibility: Control who can see your shared recipes and profile
  • Data Sharing: Opt out of usage analytics where technically feasible
  • Marketing Communications: Unsubscribe from promotional emails

7. Children's Privacy

  • Our service is not intended for children under 13 years of age
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13
  • If we learn that we have collected such information, we will delete it promptly

8. Third-Party Services

Our application integrates with the following third-party services:

  • RevenueCat: Subscription management and payment processing
  • Railway: Cloud hosting and database services
  • Expo: Mobile application development platform

These services have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them.

9. International Users

  • Our servers are located in the United States
  • By using our service, you consent to the transfer of your information to the United States
  • We ensure appropriate safeguards for international data transfers
  • Users in the European Union have rights under GDPR including data portability and erasure
  • California users have rights under CCPA including data disclosure and deletion

10. Data Retention

  • Account Data: Retained while your account is active
  • Usage Data: Anonymized after 2 years
  • Shared Content: Retained until you delete it or close your account
  • Backup Data: Securely deleted within 30 days of account deletion

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or for legal, regulatory, or operational reasons. Material changes will be communicated through the application or via email. Your continued use of our services after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us:

  • Email: privacy@yeschefapp.com
  • In-App Contact: Use the "Contact Support" feature in application settings
