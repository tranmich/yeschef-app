## 📱 How to Take Each Screenshot

### **On iOS:**
1. Open YesChef app
2. Navigate to the screen you want
3. Press **Power + Volume Up** simultaneously
4. Screenshot saves to Photos
5. AirDrop or email to computer

### **On Android:**
1. Open YesChef app
2. Navigate to the screen you want
3. Press **Power + Volume Down** simultaneously
4. Screenshot saves to Gallery
5. Transfer to computer via USB or cloud

### **From Emulator/Simulator:**
- **iOS Simulator:** `Cmd + S`
- **Android Emulator:** Click camera icon in toolbar
