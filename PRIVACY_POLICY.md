# Privacy Policy for YesChef

**Effective Date:** September 29, 2025  
**Last Updated:** September 29, 2025

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to YesChef ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and related services (collectively, the "Service").

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Personal Information You Provide
- **Account Information**: Email address, username, and password when you create an account
- **Profile Information**: Custom avatar selections (background and icon preferences)
- **Recipe Data**: Recipes you save, create, or share with the community
- **Meal Plans**: Your meal planning preferences and schedules
- **Grocery Lists**: Shopping lists you create and manage
- **Community Content**: Posts, comments, and shared recipes you contribute

### 2.2 Automatically Collected Information
- **Usage Data**: How you interact with our app (features used, time spent)
- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system, app version
- **Performance Data**: Crash reports and app performance metrics (via Expo)

### 2.3 Information from Third Parties
- **Authentication Services**: Profile information if you sign in with Google or other third-party services
- **Payment Processing**: Subscription status and payment information (via RevenueCat, when premium features are activated)

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:
- **Provide Services**: Enable core app functionality (recipes, meal planning, grocery lists)
- **Personalization**: Customize your experience with saved preferences and recommendations
- **Community Features**: Enable recipe sharing and social interactions with friends
- **Account Management**: Authenticate your account and provide customer support
- **Improve Our Service**: Analyze usage patterns to enhance app performance and features
- **Premium Features**: Manage subscription status and premium feature access
- **Communication**: Send important updates about your account or the service

## 4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

### 4.1 We Do NOT Sell Your Personal Information

### 4.2 We May Share Information:
- **With Your Consent**: When you explicitly choose to share content publicly
- **Service Providers**: 
  - Railway (cloud hosting and database)
  - Expo (mobile app platform)
  - RevenueCat (subscription management)
  - OpenAI (AI-powered recipe features, when implemented)
- **Legal Requirements**: When required by law or to protect our rights
- **Business Transfers**: In connection with mergers, acquisitions, or asset sales

### 4.3 Community Features
- **Recipe Sharing**: Content you choose to share publicly is visible to other users
- **Friend Connections**: Your username and shared recipes are visible to accepted friends
- **Custom Avatars**: Your selected avatar appears with your shared content

## 5. Data Storage and Security

### 5.1 Data Storage
- **Primary Storage**: Your data is securely stored on Railway cloud infrastructure
- **Local Storage**: Some preferences are cached locally on your device for offline access
- **Backup Systems**: Regular backups ensure data recovery capabilities

### 5.2 Security Measures
- **Encryption**: Data transmission is encrypted using industry-standard protocols
- **Authentication**: Secure login with hashed passwords
- **Access Controls**: Limited access to personal data by authorized personnel only
- **Regular Updates**: Security patches and updates are applied promptly

## 6. Your Rights and Choices

### 6.1 Account Control
- **Access**: View and update your profile information anytime
- **Data Portability**: Request a copy of your data in a portable format
- **Deletion**: Delete your account and associated data through the app settings

### 6.2 Privacy Settings
- **Profile Visibility**: Control who can see your shared recipes and profile
- **Data Sharing**: Opt out of usage analytics (where technically feasible)
- **Marketing Communications**: Unsubscribe from promotional emails

### 6.3 Children's Privacy
- Our service is not intended for children under 13
- We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13
- If we learn we have collected such information, we will delete it promptly

## 7. Cookies and Tracking

### 7.1 Local Storage
- **Preferences**: We store app settings and preferences locally on your device
- **Authentication**: Session tokens for maintaining your login status
- **Offline Data**: Cached recipes and meal plans for offline access

### 7.2 Analytics
- **Expo Analytics**: Basic usage statistics to improve app performance
- **Crash Reporting**: Automatic crash reports to identify and fix bugs
- **No Third-Party Tracking**: We do not use advertising trackers or sell data to advertisers

## 8. International Users

### 8.2 Regional Rights

- **Other Regions**: Local privacy rights as applicable
