1. Introduction

Welcome to YesChef ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and related services.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Email address, username, and password
  • Profile Information: Custom avatar selections and preferences
  • Recipe Data: Recipes you save, create, or share
  • Meal Plans: Your meal planning preferences and schedules
  • Grocery Lists: Shopping lists you create and manage
  • Community Content: Posts, comments, and shared recipes

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Usage Data: How you interact with our app
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, app version
  • Performance Data: Crash reports and app performance metrics

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide Services: Enable core app functionality
  • Personalization: Customize your experience
  • Community Features: Enable recipe sharing and social interactions
  • Account Management: Authenticate your account and provide support
  • Improve Our Service: Analyze usage patterns to enhance performance
  • Premium Features: Manage subscription status and feature access

4. Information Sharing

❌ We Do NOT Sell Your Personal Information

We May Share Information:

  • With Your Consent: When you choose to share content publicly
  • Service Providers: Railway (hosting), Expo (mobile platform), RevenueCat (subscriptions)
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law

5. Data Security

  • Encryption: Data transmission is encrypted
  • Authentication: Secure login with hashed passwords
  • Access Controls: Limited access to personal data
  • Regular Updates: Security patches applied promptly

6. Your Rights

  • Access: View and update your profile information
  • Data Portability: Request a copy of your data
  • Deletion: Delete your account and data through app settings
  • Privacy Controls: Control who sees your shared content

7. Children's Privacy

  • Our service is not intended for children under 13
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13
  • If we learn we have collected such information, we will delete it promptly

8. Third-Party Services

  • RevenueCat: Subscription management
  • Railway: Cloud hosting and database
  • Expo: Mobile platform

9. Data Retention

  • Account Data: Retained while your account is active
  • Usage Data: Anonymized after 2 years
  • Shared Content: Retained until you delete it
  • Backup Data: Securely deleted within 30 days of account deletion

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Material changes will be notified through the app or via email. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
